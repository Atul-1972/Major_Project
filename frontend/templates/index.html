      Privacy & screening limitations +

      This research prototype is not a medical diagnosis or a replacement for professional care. Technical image checks cannot determine whether an image depicts skin or matches the training domain.

      No accounts or screening history are stored. Images are not sent to third-party AI services. Clearing the selection releases its local preview; cancelling a request does not guarantee the server has stopped processing it.

      Grad-CAM remains unavailable until its implementation and branch/layer scope are validated.
